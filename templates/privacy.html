Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your account details and the expenses you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as browser type and basic usage data, to help keep the service working smoothly. We do not collect full bank account or card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and operate the Spendly service, to display your expenses and spending summaries, and to improve the features and reliability of the application.

We do not sell your personal data. We may use aggregated, non-identifying information to understand how the service is used and to make it better.

3. Data Storage

Your information is stored so that it can be shown back to you when you use Spendly. We take reasonable measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials safe and should avoid entering highly sensitive information.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third party services, such as web hosting and font delivery, to operate the application. These providers may process limited technical information as necessary to deliver their services.

We do not share your expense data with third parties for marketing purposes. Any third party service providers are expected to handle information in a manner consistent with this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the Spendly application.
